All three distractors (4.4, 5.0, 5.7) correspond to acidic pH values typical of lysosomal, vacuolar, or secreted enzymes, which are inconsistent with a cytosolic SDR lacking targeting signals. This reasoning meaningfully eliminates B, C, and D and favors A.
