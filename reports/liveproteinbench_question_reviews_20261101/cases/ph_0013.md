### Reasoning versus lookup: reasoning-supported

**Discriminating biological argument**

> The protein is a plant enzyme involved in monoterpene indole alkaloid (MIA) biosynthesis. MIA biosynthesis occurs in the cytoplasm of plant cells, where pH is approximately 7.2. The sequence lacks a cleavable N-terminal signal peptide or transmembrane helices that would indicate secretion into an acidic compartment (vacuole, lysosome-like organelle). A cytoplasmic enzyme is expected to have optimal pH near neutral (6-8), not in the strongly acidic range (5.0-5.7). This compartment-based reasoning eliminates options A (5.7), C (5.0), and D (5.6) in favor of B (6.0-8.0).


**Missing item-specific fact**

> The exact experimentally measured optimum pH range of 6-8 for this specific AsAKS2 variant comes from Wang et al. 2022 (Chem. Sci. 13:12389). A purely sequence-based predictor could not produce this precise range without training data or structural modeling.


**Reason**

> The key discriminator is whether the enzyme operates in a near-neutral cytoplasmic environment or an acidic compartment. General knowledge of plant MIA biosynthesis being cytoplasmic, combined with the absence of targeting signals in the sequence, provides a defensible biological argument to prefer the near-neutral range (B) over the three acidic single-value options (A, C, D). The three acidic distractors are mutually indistinguishable by reasoning, but they are collectively eliminable by the compartment argument.
