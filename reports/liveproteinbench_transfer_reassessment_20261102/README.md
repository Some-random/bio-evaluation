# LiveProteinBench: transfer-knowledge reassessment

**1,328 selected questions; both models reassessed the same cohort.** Selection is the union of prior format-valid lookup-dependent or uncertain reviews. The [408 other admitted questions](NOT_REASSESSED.md) were not reassessed. The earlier 61 source-question exclusions remain outside the original admitted 1,736.

**Usable output means format-valid review.** It is separate from the actual answerability classification: a usable output can say **reasoning-supported, lookup-dependent or uncertain**. It does not certify biological correctness. An **output error** counts a model response that cannot be used as a structured review, not a defective question or wrong author key.

The revised instruction permits credible inference from related proteins, families, homologs, structure, mechanisms and learned biological patterns, while requiring actual discrimination among the offered choices. Exact-protein answer recall remains lookup. Keys, labels and output schema are unchanged.

Round-specific pairs with two usable outputs are separately reported in the summary and are not used as a shifting comparison denominator.

**AI judgments, not expert adjudication.** Agreement is not proof of correctness. Reviewers saw the author key and reviewer-only evidence; original benchmark scores are unchanged. A revised label measures a judgment under the clarified prompt, not a directly verified biological fact or a causal effect isolated from sampling variation.
