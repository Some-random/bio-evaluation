Arbitrary explanatory prose is not silently converted to a medical answer. Free-text extraction requires an explicit terminal answer field. It never inspects hidden reasoning or references.

`run_visible_extraction.py` now uses **provenance v2**: it opens and hashes the actual original contract and run manifest, requires their identity/hash linkage, and compares the exact ordered full case list. Each case binds its original request, payload, response, raw body and saved-final hashes, model/revision and protocol. OPEN, CLOSED and MCQ cases use their own original question-type rule; one global rule cannot silently replace that mapping. A self-asserted verification boolean is not accepted. Empty or absent finals remain invalid; nonempty conflicts fail validation.
