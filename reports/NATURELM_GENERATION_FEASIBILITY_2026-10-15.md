P37 and P38 remain valid biological generation tasks, but neither has a recovered native execution contract sufficient for a paper-matched run. This audit inspected public author sources only. No generation, oracle forward, training, checkpoint download or execution authorization occurred. Existing frozen files remain unchanged.

These facts and rounded comparators come from [NatureLM Sections 4.1–4.2, Tables 8–9 and Figure S16](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.07527v3#S4.SS1). The 1,000 count describes the **natural reference**, not a verified generation budget. Table 8 and later prose report different pLDDT values/conditions; this audit retains the table comparator without choosing a more favorable value. Solubility remains graphical, without invented numerical estimates.

The [pinned author repository](https://github.com/microsoft/SFM/tree/bcb1351bc6490d4a98cae74e7eead30a028fae00/NatureLM) contains seven NatureLM files: two inference notebooks, a README and packaging files. The notebook generation settings—100 output tokens and temperature 0.7—demonstrate molecule generation and aspirin translation. They do not establish the protein experiments’ budgets. Complete model-tree metadata for the linked publisher [base](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/NatureLM-8x7B/tree/e1150df2e00f819b87de473d6af5c2105b21ccaa) and [instruction](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/NatureLM-8x7B-Inst/tree/fcef847f23e2ae146ff4d77b434f6b775b594407) releases identifies generator weights and tokenizer files, without the evaluation cohorts or property heads. Both releases are public; their weights total about 93.4 and 93.5 GB respectively. Neither was downloaded, and a Qwen adaptation would reuse the existing Qwen checkpoint.

The full Figure S16 prompt inventory was recovered as source provenance. It has no recovered sampling allocation, order, seed or per-prompt count, so it was not converted into benchmark cases. No author sample outputs, exact UR50 subset or dedicated classifier artifact was found in the inspected publisher locations. This is a bounded source audit, not a claim that no such artifact exists anywhere.

The cited [SFM-Protein Sections 4.3–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24022#S4.SS3) distinguish continuous stability regression from solubility classification. They do not identify the NatureLM binary stability oracle’s trained checkpoint, label conversion or probability orientation. Training a new head or substituting another predictor would change the reference metric.

[OmegaFold’s pinned public release](https://github.com/HeliXonProtein/OmegaFold/tree/313c873ad190b64506a497c926649e15fcd88fcd) supplies code and links to model 1/2 weights. Its available settings include cycles, pseudo-MSA masking and precision. The NatureLM evaluator’s choices remain unverified. OmegaFold also exposes a weighted overall confidence, while the paper’s average pLDDT does not resolve that aggregation versus a simple residue mean. No weights were acquired. Any future installation must use a new environment and preserve the frozen P42 runtime.

Some **qualified partial metrics** are technically possible after a concrete protocol is approved: P37 length statistics require no oracle, and declared clustering/folding metrics could follow after their settings and assets are fixed. The missing native sample count and validity rules currently prevent an exact-budget comparison. P38’s native property metrics need the actual trained classifiers; length or heuristic solubility scores would not substitute for them. No arbitrary 1,000-generation cohort, replacement natural reference or context ablation has been created.

Compute can presently be estimated only symbolically. With one completion per slot, generation requires `N37 + Nstable + Nsoluble + Ninsoluble` requests and output work bounded by the corresponding `N × token_limit` sums; those native quantities are unknown. P37 requires one folding evaluation per eligible short output. P38 would require generated property evaluations plus 2,000 natural-reference evaluations across the two classifiers. API charges are $0 for local execution; wall-clock and hardware cost remain unspecified because no timing forward or verified workload exists.

These tasks may demand learned protein representations and sequence/structure regularities. Good scores would establish properties of the frozen output cohort under the specified evaluators; they would not validate a rationale or establish measured biological function. Bad scores could reflect delivery, generation or evaluator agreement under the declared adaptation and would not isolate a model’s internal reasoning.
